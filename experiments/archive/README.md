# Historical experiment archive

This directory preserves exploratory or legacy branches that could not safely be designated as the canonical AMFormer pipeline.

- `amformer_variants/` and `amformerv2/`: architectural/training variants and original outputs.
- `baseline_variants/`: successive FT-Transformer scripts.
- `pga/`, `ablation/`, and `dynamic_model.py`: adjacent model families and ablations.
- `lasso/`: feature-selection experiments.
- `web_prototype/`: application prototype and integration code.
- Thesis, preprocessing, plotting, document, and bundled assets: historical research materials.

Nothing here is designated clinically validated or production-ready. Archived paths are left unchanged when rewriting them would obscure provenance or risk historical behavior.
